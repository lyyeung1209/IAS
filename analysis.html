Choose AI with care.
Know the risks.

IAS aims to make AI agent details easier to find and check. It plans to bring information, A–C labels, and user reports together. The service is not live. Its demand, rating rules, and income plan still need testing.

USER VALUEMARKET HYPOTHESISRISKS / LIMITS
01 VALUE & TRADE-OFFS

More information helps.
Good checks matter too.

IAS aims to help people compare AI agents, save search time, and find reports about possible scams. These are expected benefits, not measured results.

↗POSSIBLE BENEFITS

How people could benefit

  • 01
    Compare in one placeSee what a service does and where its public details come from.
  • 02
    Find clues soonerBring user reports, expert views, and public details together.
  • 03
    Share useful trendsPlan regular reports for users and groups that study AI.
!RISKS

What could go wrong

  • 01
    False or unfair reportsPeople may misuse reports. A wrong label can harm users and services.
  • 02
    Privacy and securityReports and screenshots need clear rules for access and storage.
  • 03
    Bias or too much trustBad data or over-trusting a label can lead to unfair or poor choices.
⌁LIMITS

What IAS cannot promise

  • 01
    More users mean more dataFew users or reports can make results weak or incomplete.
  • 02
    Not a legal or safety checkRules and services change. A label cannot replace expert advice.
  • 03
    Costs need a planTechnology, staff time, and careful data checks all cost money.

03 A–C LABELS

A label is not a promise.
It is a place to start checking.

The project plans to use A–C labels for AI agent details. These draft labels need clear rules, good evidence, and a way to challenge mistakes before use.

MORE DETAILSNEEDS CHECKINGNOT ENOUGH INFO
ASOURCES CHECKED

Sources mostly match

Public details look consistent. This does not mean the service is safe or legal.

BMORE CHECKS NEEDED

Some details are unclear

Sources may be missing or do not match. Check the evidence and date.

CNOT ENOUGH INFORMATION

Source cannot be checked

There is not enough information to check the source. This is not a finding of wrongdoing.

i

Please remember A–C labels are only an idea for this project. They are not real IAS ratings. Missing details do not mean a service is harmful. Check the sources, dates, and replies. Do not decide based on one letter alone.
