01 THE IAS IDEA

From search to reports,
make choices with more information.

Details about AI agents are spread across many websites. IAS aims to bring public information and user reports together, so people can compare services and see what is still unknown.

◈01 — DISCOVER

Find AI agent details

See what a service does and where its public information comes from.

↗
⌖02 — LOCAL RULES

Check local rules

Rules can differ by place. Experts should review any legal guidance.

↗
⌘03 — A–C LABEL

Compare information

A planned A–C label would show what is known, not promise that a service is safe.

↗
↗04 — COMMUNITY

Send feedback or a report

User reports could help flag issues, but each report needs a careful review.

↗
02 BUILT FOR BETTER CHOICES

We do not endorse agents.
We make it easier to check the facts.

IAS is planned for people who use AI agents and groups that study AI safety. Its results would be clues, not final answers. Reports can be wrong or misused, so important claims need evidence and human review.
